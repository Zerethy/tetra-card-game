How to Play

  1. Each duelist shuffles a deck and draws 5 cards into a hand.
  2. On your turn, select a card, then click an empty square on the 3×3 board.
  3. Hover any face-up card — especially ones already on the board — to enlarge its ranks, name, level, and art. The preview is click-through.
  4. A placed card compares its touching side ranks with every orthogonally adjacent enemy (Top vs Bottom, Left vs Right).
  5. Higher number captures — the stronger touching side flips the weaker card, even if the weaker card was just placed. Ties do nothing unless an element tie-break decides it. The table flashes 8 vs 3 — capture on every clash.
  6. Captured cards can combo — they immediately compare their other sides against remaining neighbors.
  7. The board fills at 9 cards. Highest total of board + remaining hand wins.
  8. A fresh album is your identity plus level-1 beasts. Winning a stage unlocks the next rival (1 → 10).
  9. Open the Album. Your five sit at the top. Spare owned cards stay listed under Switch with these (strongest / newest first). Tap a spare then a slot, or a slot then a spare. After a win, the album opens on your new card. Identity stays pinned. Browse all opens the full collection.

Cards are Levels 1–10 (beasts through sovereigns). Boss 1 uses weak-tier Lv1 signatures, beatable with starters. Mid bosses step up and add one stronger ultimate. Boss 10 keeps a full late kit and is hard, not unwinnable. Each card prints four ranks on its sides (1–A). Clashes are higher number wins (6 beats 3). Elements follow the three wheels on the plaque (tie-break only; never overturn a number gap). After a win, Move On claims the trade — that is how you earn stronger ultimates. Death Match is not required after a clean win — only at one card, or if you opt in for bigger stakes.
