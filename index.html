Aetherbound

Draw five dark-fantasy champions, place them on the ninefold grid, and capture your rival by placing adjacent and beating the touching side ranks.

After a duel

How to Play

  1. Each duelist shuffles a deck and draws 5 cards into a hand.
  2. On your turn, select a card, then click an empty square on the 3×3 board.
  3. A placed card compares its touching side ranks with every orthogonally adjacent enemy (Top vs Bottom, Left vs Right).
  4. Higher number captures and flips the enemy card to your color. Ties do nothing. Losing a compare does not cost your card.
  5. Captured cards can combo — they immediately compare their other sides against remaining neighbors.
  6. The board fills at 9 cards. Highest total of board + remaining hand wins.

Cards are Levels 1–10 (beasts through sovereigns). Each starter deck mixes mostly 1–5s with a warlord, a relic, and one sovereign. Each card prints four ranks on its sides (1–A). Clashes are higher number wins (6 beats 3). Elements follow the three wheels on the plaque (tie-break only; never overturn a number gap). After a win, Move On claims the trade. Death Match is not required after a clean win — only at one card, or if you opt in for bigger stakes.
