Aetherbound

Draw five dark-fantasy champions, place them on the ninefold grid, and capture your rival with arrows, clashes, and combos.

How to Play

  1. Each duelist shuffles a deck and draws 5 cards into a hand.
  2. On your turn, select a card, then click an empty square on the 3×3 board.
  3. Arrows on the card attack adjacent enemy cards (including diagonals).
  4. No arrow pointing back: automatic capture (the card flips to your color).
  5. Arrows clash: a battle uses Attack vs the defense your battle type names. Winner keeps the loser.
  6. Captured cards can combo—their arrows flip nearby enemy cards without another fight.
  7. The board fills at 9 cards. Highest total of board + remaining hand wins.

P Physical vs P.Def · M Magical vs M.Def · X Flexible vs lower defense · A Assault vs the defender’s lowest stat. Elements add a small advantage on the elemental wheel.
